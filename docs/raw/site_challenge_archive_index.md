## 2025 BEHAVIOR Challenge

*Foundation Models Meet Embodied Agents Challenge @ NeurIPS 2025*

The 1st BEHAVIOR Challenge tasked participants with solving 50 full-length household tasks in the BEHAVIOR-1K environment, supported by 10,000 teleoperated expert demonstrations (1,200+ hours).
